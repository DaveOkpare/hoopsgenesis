# Ogenesis

Ogenesis is a sports academy operating across countries and cities. These terms describe its people, portals, and locations.

## Language

**Student**:
A person who participates in academy training.

**Parent**:
A parent or guardian of a student.

**Parent–student link**:
An established relationship between a parent and a student. A parent can be linked to multiple students, and a student can be linked to multiple parents.

**Link code**:
A code shared from an existing student's profile to establish a parent–student link.
_Avoid_: Referral code

**Coach**:
A person who delivers academy training.

**Admin**:
A staff member who manages academy operations at assigned locations within a country.

**Superuser**:
A staff member who can manage academy operations at assigned locations across countries.

**Role**:
A person's assigned capacity when using Ogenesis: student, parent, coach, admin, or superuser. Each person has one role in the first version.

**Portal**:
An area of Ogenesis serving a particular audience. The three audiences are parents and students, coaches, and admins and superusers.

**Location**:
A physical academy branch or training site. A country or city can contain several locations.
_Avoid_: Country, region, tenant

**Location access**:
The specific academy locations in which a person is allowed to use their assigned role. Sharing a country or city with an assigned location does not grant access to other locations there.

**Team**:
A training group based at one academy location. Students and coaches can each participate in multiple teams.

**Team membership**:
A student's or coach's participation in a particular team. A parent's connection to a team comes through their linked student rather than direct participation.
